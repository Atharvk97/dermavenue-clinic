Privacy Policy

At DermAvenue Skin & Hair Clinic, the privacy of our patients is of paramount importance. All personal information collected during registration, consultation, or treatment — including name, contact details, medical history, and treatment records — is held in strict confidence and used solely for the purpose of providing medical care.

We do not sell, share, or disclose patient information to any third party, except where required by law or with the explicit written consent of the patient. Digital records are stored on secure, access-controlled systems and are accessible only to authorised clinical staff involved in your care.

Patients have the right to request a copy of their personal data at any time, and to request corrections or deletion of non-essential data, subject to applicable medical record-keeping obligations under Indian law.

This policy is governed by the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011, and all applicable Indian healthcare regulations. By consulting at DermAvenue, you acknowledge and consent to this policy.

Patient Data Confidentiality

DermAvenue Skin & Hair Clinic is committed to maintaining the highest standards of patient data confidentiality. All clinical records, photographs, investigation reports, prescription data, and personal identifiers are treated as strictly confidential medical information.

Patient records are never shared with any external party — including pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, or media — without the patient's explicit prior written consent. Clinical photographs used for treatment progress documentation are stored securely and are not published or shared without express approval.

Staff members are bound by confidentiality agreements and receive regular training on data protection protocols. Access to patient files is strictly limited to treating clinical personnel on a need-to-know basis.

In compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable healthcare regulations, all digital data is encrypted and stored on secure, access-controlled servers. Breach incidents, if any, will be disclosed to affected patients in accordance with legal requirements.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Consultation fees are non-refundable once a consultation has been conducted. The consultation fee of ₹1,500 covers the clinical assessment, diagnosis, and treatment planning provided by Dr. Ajay Nimbalkar during the appointment.

Appointments cancelled at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled time may be rescheduled without penalty, subject to availability. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may result in forfeiture of any advance payment or booking deposit.

For procedural treatments (e.g., laser sessions, chemical peels, hair transplant procedures), a treatment deposit may be collected at the time of booking. Refunds on procedural deposits are considered on a case-by-case basis at the clinic's discretion, particularly in cases of documented medical contraindications.

To cancel or reschedule an appointment, please contact the clinic directly via WhatsApp or phone at 09665669966 at the earliest convenience.

LGBTQ+ Patient Charter

DermAvenue Skin & Hair Clinic is a safe, inclusive, and affirming space for all patients, regardless of sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, or relationship structure. Every patient who walks through our doors deserves — and will receive — equal, respectful, and compassionate care.

Dr. Ajay Nimbalkar and our clinical team are committed to providing non-judgmental dermatological and aesthetic care to LGBTQ+ individuals. We understand that healthcare experiences can be fraught with anxiety for many members of the community, and we are dedicated to making every consultation feel safe and dignified.

Patients are welcome to use their preferred name and pronouns at all times. Clinical decisions are made purely on the basis of medical need and patient preferences — never on the basis of identity. Transgender and non-binary patients seeking gender-affirming skincare, laser hair removal, or aesthetic treatments will receive the same standard of excellence as all our patients.

Discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited within our clinic premises and will not be tolerated from staff, patients, or visitors. We encourage open communication and invite all patients to share any concerns about their care experience with us directly.
